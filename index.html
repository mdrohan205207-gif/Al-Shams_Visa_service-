📚 Australia Visa Guide

Last checked: November 2026. Rules, fees and processing times change often, so always confirm on the official Department of Home Affairs website (immi.homeaffairs.gov.au).

🧳 Visitor visa (subclass 600): tourism, family visit, business
  • Who it is for: short trips for tourism, visiting family or friends, or short business visits such as meetings and conferences.
  • Streams: Tourist, Business Visitor and Sponsored Family. Some passports can use a simpler ETA (601) or eVisitor (651) instead.
  • You cannot work on a visitor visa, including paid or remote work for an Australian employer.
  • Stay: usually granted for 3, 6 or 12 months depending on your situation and evidence.
  • Key test: you must show you are a genuine temporary visitor: a clear purpose, enough funds, strong ties to your home country (job, business, family, property) and a plan to leave on time.
  • Timing: Home Affairs figures (Sep 2026) show about half of Tourist stream applications decided within 18 days and 90% within 38 days. Sponsored Family usually takes longer. These are guides, not promises.
  • Tip: do not book non-refundable flights or hotels before the visa is granted.
  • New rule: for applications lodged from 28 November 2026, the "No Further Stay" condition is mandatory on subclass 600, 601 and 651. In general you cannot apply for another visa while you are in Australia, so plan to apply for any later visa from outside Australia.
🎓 Student visa (subclass 500): study in Australia
  • Core requirements: enrolment in a registered course (Confirmation of Enrolment, CoE), Genuine Student requirement, enough funds, English language evidence, health and character checks, and Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC).
  • Genuine Student statement: explain clearly why this course, this provider and Australia, how it fits your past study or work, and how it helps your future career. Generic or copied answers are a common cause of refusal.
  • Funds: you must show you can pay tuition, living costs and travel. The living-cost figure is updated by the government (around AUD 29,710 per year was the figure in recent guidance, so check the current amount). The source of funds must be clear and genuine.
  • English: a recognised test result is generally required (for example IELTS). Evidence levels can be stricter depending on your passport country and provider, so check your personal document checklist.
  • Switching: visitor visa and Temporary Graduate visa holders generally cannot switch to a student visa from inside Australia and must apply from overseas.
  • Note: student visa refusal rates have been high for some countries recently, so a complete, consistent and honest application matters a lot.
💼 Work visas: employer-sponsored and skilled
  • Employer-sponsored (subclass 482, Skills in Demand): you need a job offer from an approved Australian employer, the right skills and experience for the occupation, and English ability.
  • Skilled migration (such as 189, 190, 491) is points-based and usually needs a skills assessment and an invitation.
  • Timing: work visas often take months, depending on the stream and occupation. Home Affairs publishes current processing times.
  • Be careful: do not pay anyone who promises a guaranteed job or visa. Genuine jobs come from approved employers.
👨‍👩‍👧 Family and partner visas
  • These need an eligible Australian sponsor (citizen, permanent resident or eligible New Zealand citizen) and strong evidence that the relationship is genuine and continuing.
  • Evidence usually includes identity documents, relationship history, shared finances or commitments, photos and statements from family and friends.
  • Processing can take a long time and fees are high. Get your documents organised before lodging.

⚠️ Common reasons for refusal
  • Unclear travel or study purpose, or answers that do not match your documents.
  • Not enough funds, or large unexplained deposits in bank statements.
  • Weak ties to your home country (no job, business, family or property).
  • Previous visa refusals or overstays that are not explained.
  • Incomplete forms, missing documents or missed requests for biometrics, medical checks or police certificates.
  • False or altered documents can lead to refusal and a ban from future visas. We only work with genuine documents.
✅ Smart tips before you apply
  • Apply early and check your passport has enough validity.
  • Use your personal document checklist on the official Home Affairs website, because requirements depend on your passport, visa type and where you apply.
  • Keep documents clear, recent and consistent: same name, dates and details everywhere.
  • Answer every question honestly and declare any previous refusals.
  • Government visa charges are paid to the Australian Government and are separate from our service fee. Ask for a receipt for every payment.
  • We will never ask for your passwords or one-time codes (OTP).
